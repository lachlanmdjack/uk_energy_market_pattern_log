## Cross Commodity Futures

### NBP Gas Futures and Spark Spread

- Following ICE's UK NBP Natural Gas Futures for Q4, prices have continued to rise to a 4-month high and rose throughout the day. One piece of critical thinking is that the conflict over the last week has led the situation to devolve to a state similar to the beginning of the conflict - which peaked at 149p/Th and prices are just increasing through 140p/Th on this Monday. Therefore, one may want to argue that the 150p/Th has been the recent ceiling without any long term resolution so prices shouldn't increase past that again. However, to argue against this, Europe's storage levels are only at 54%, basically the lowest in the last 5 years. Where there is mandate and obligation to fill further than this, it will likely enable Europe to compete with Asia for near-term LNG, pushing futures prices up beyond levels seen months ago.
